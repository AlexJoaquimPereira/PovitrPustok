Zori ap-aplea akara ani vorga pormannem dôr Stotrak khaxa nirôp asa, tori sogllim Stotram ekach tallean ho somsarko nirôp ditat: Iave (Sorvespora), Tum amcho mogacho Dev. Ho ak’khê Judevanchê, Devan vinchun kaddlolê Israel-porjecho disachea-disa kallzachea ghonddeantlo bhavarth, bhorvanso ani magnnem. Iaven Israel-porjek Ejiptantli soddoiloli ani Apli porza kel’li: tôr hi porza sodankal Tachi, ani tê poilê soddvonnê khatir sodam tichi soddvonn korunk Dev Israel-porjek Apunnuch bandon ravlo. Devachi stuti korun gitam Taka ubarunk hench karonn, veg-vegllea akantam ani odruxttam fuddem Taka vinoti korunk hench karonn, Tachea denneam-upkaram khatir Taka argam diunk hench karonn.
